Shipping Policy

Last Updated: March 7, 2026

Thank you for shopping with Artgift. We strive to deliver your orders safely and as quickly as possible.

Free Shipping

We offer Free Shipping on all prepaid orders across India.

There are no additional shipping charges for prepaid purchases.

Order Processing Time

Artwork Creation Time

Since some of our products may involve custom preparation or design, the artwork creation process usually takes 2–3 business days after the order is placed.

Printing and Shipping Time

After the artwork is finalized, we will print and dispatch your order within 7–9 business days.

Delivery Time

Delivery time may vary depending on your location and the courier service used. Typically, orders are delivered within 3–7 business days after dispatch.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you may receive tracking details via email or SMS so you can monitor the delivery status of your order.

Possible Delivery Delays

While we try our best to meet the estimated timelines, delivery delays may occasionally occur due to factors such as:

  • Courier partner delays
  • Weather conditions
  • Public holidays
  • Unexpected logistical issues

Artgift is not responsible for delays caused by third-party shipping providers, but we will assist you in tracking your order whenever possible.
