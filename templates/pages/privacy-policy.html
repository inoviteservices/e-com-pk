Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 7, 2026

At Artgift, accessible from artgift.in, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or purchase our products.

By accessing or using our website, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you visit our website, place an order, or contact us, we may collect certain personal information including:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Shipping Address
  • Billing Address
  • Payment Information
  • Order Details

This information is collected when you voluntarily provide it to us.

Non-Personal Information

We may automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing activity such as:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • Pages visited on our website
  • Time spent on pages

This information may be collected using cookies and similar tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

To Process Orders

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Manage payments and invoices
  • Provide order updates and confirmations

Customer Support

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Provide support related to orders or services

Website Improvement

  • Analyze user behavior
  • Improve website functionality and user experience

Marketing Communications

With your consent, we may send promotional emails regarding:

  • New products
  • Special offers
  • Updates or announcements

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

However, we may share your information in the following cases:

Service Providers

We may share data with trusted third parties such as:

  • Payment gateways
  • Shipping partners
  • Website hosting providers

These partners only receive information necessary to perform their services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

  • Court orders
  • Legal processes
  • Government requests

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies to:

  • Improve user experience
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze website traffic

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, although some website features may not function properly.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

However, please note that no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing emails

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Artgift reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with the updated effective date.

We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
